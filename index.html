Our Aim

Evidence-based understanding of AI's impact

The aim of the Association is to monitor, assess, and promote understanding of the impacts of Artificial Intelligence (AI) on society and individuals. In pursuing this aim, the Association seeks to raise awareness of the risks and impacts associated with AI and to disseminate reliable, evidence-based information that enables individuals and society to make informed decisions regarding its use.

“Wisdom is rooted in truth, experience, deep understanding, and sound judgment.”
The Principle of Wisdom (SOPHIA)

The Association is founded upon the belief that wisdom is rooted in truth, experience, deep understanding, and sound judgment.

By promoting a clear understanding of the impacts of Artificial Intelligence (AI) on individuals and society, SOPHIA seeks to equip individuals, institutions, and communities with the knowledge necessary to make informed decisions for the benefit of present and future generations.

Objectives

To achieve its aim, the Association shall

01

Monitor and assess the impacts of Artificial Intelligence on individuals, society, and key societal sectors, with particular attention to emerging risks and challenges.

02

Conduct and promote research on the impacts of Artificial Intelligence across key societal sectors, including education, healthcare, employment, media, tourism, psychology, and other fields of public interest.

03

Raise public awareness and understanding of the impacts and risks associated with Artificial Intelligence through publications, educational initiatives, conferences, seminars, workshops, and other awareness-raising activities.

04

Facilitate dialogue and collaboration among researchers, professionals, public authorities, educational institutions, civil society organisations, industry, and other relevant stakeholders to promote informed discussion and knowledge exchange on the impacts of Artificial Intelligence.
